# GymCoach Music Directory

Organize your workout music by day of the week:

```
music/
├── monday/       # Tracks for Monday workout (e.g. Chest / Push A)
├── tuesday/      # Tracks for Tuesday workout (e.g. Back / Pull A)
├── wednesday/    # Tracks for Wednesday workout (e.g. Legs / Core)
├── thursday/     # Tracks for Thursday workout (e.g. Shoulders / Push B)
├── friday/       # Tracks for Friday workout (e.g. Arms / Pull B)
├── saturday/     # Tracks for Saturday workout (e.g. Full Body / Cardio)
└── sunday/       # Tracks for Sunday workout (e.g. Mobility / Rest)
```

## Supported Formats
- `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.ogg`, `.flac`

## How it works
1. **YouTube to MP3 Extractor**: In GymCoach **Settings ➜ Workout Music**, paste any YouTube link, choose the target weekday, and click **Extract & Add**. The audio is saved directly to your browser player and downloaded into this folder.
2. **Direct Upload**: You can also drag-and-drop or upload songs directly in **Settings ➜ Workout Music**.
3. **In-Page Player**: During your workout, GymCoach automatically plays the playlist for the current day!
