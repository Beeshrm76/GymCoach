Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you create an account, we collect only essential information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Data: Username, email address, and an encrypted password.
  • Profile Data (Optional): Display name, bio, avatar, and height metrics.
  • Fitness Data: Workouts, sets, reps, logs, and body weight entries that you actively record.

We do not collect background location, tracking cookies for advertising, or data via third-party marketing SDKs. Our application is strictly non-revenue.

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the GymCoach service. We store your data securely via Supabase and allow you to sync across devices. We will never sell your data to third parties.

GymCoach is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us at the email below to request immediate data deletion.

4. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion directly within the application (via the User Profile settings), which will irreversibly purge your account, workout history, and personal details from our active databases.

Cookie Policy

GymCoach uses minimal cookies and local storage to function securely.

  • Essential Data: We use local browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to keep you logged in, persist your offline workout data, and store your app preferences (like theme and cookie consent).
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or any advertising cookies. Your usage habits remain your own.

You can clear your local storage at any time via your browser settings, though doing so will log you out and remove any unsynced offline workouts.
